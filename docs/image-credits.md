## Missing / placeholder needed

None. Every required and optional slug was fetched.

Notes for art direction:

- `optometrist-exam`, `slit-lamp`, `phoropter`, `care-consult` and `frames-flatlay` come from bright, white-clinic shoots. On a dark, cinematic page they will need grading (lower exposure, warmer tones).
- `child-eye-test` is a school vision screening, not an in-practice exam. That suits a "school screenings / kids' eyes" message.
- More candidates are in the session scratchpad if alternates are needed (for example, a Black family on a lawn and a close-up portrait of a young Black person in tortoiseshell glasses).
